# Code of Conduct
### *Our Commitment*

The NXD project is committed to maintaining a welcoming, productive, and respectful environment for everyone participating in the project.

Contributors, maintainers, users, and community members are expected to engage with one another professionally and in good faith.

---

#### Expected Behavior

Examples of positive behavior include:

    Giving and accepting constructive technical feedback
    Respecting differing experiences, perspectives, and skill levels
    Keeping technical disagreements focused on ideas rather than individuals
    Asking questions in good faith
    Helping others understand unfamiliar concepts
    Acknowledging mistakes and correcting them
    Respecting project decisions even when you disagree with them

Technical criticism is welcome. Personal attacks are not.

---

#### Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

    Harassment, threats, or intimidation
    Personal insults or deliberately demeaning comments
    Discrimination or derogatory comments directed at individuals or groups
    Sustained disruption of project discussions
    Deliberately inflammatory or hostile behavior
    Publishing another person's private information without permission
    Unwanted sexual attention or inappropriate sexual content
    Repeated bad-faith interactions intended primarily to antagonize other participants

---

#### Technical Disagreement

Disagreement is a normal part of software and language development.

Participants are encouraged to challenge designs, implementations, proposals, and technical assumptions. Criticism should address the work or idea being discussed rather than the person presenting it.

A proposal being rejected is not a rejection of the contributor.

Final decisions regarding the direction of NXD and its official projects are made by the project maintainers.

---

#### Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to official NXD project spaces:[nxd_language](https://github.com/GTS-AppliedTech/nxd_language), [nxd-extension](https://github.com/GTS-AppliedTech/nxd-extension), [nxdlang.org](https://github.com/GTS-AppliedTech/nxdlang.org), and [nxd-rust-frontend](https://github.com/GTS-AppliedTech/nxd-rust-frontend). Including and not limited to: issue trackers, pull requests, discussions, and other community spaces managed by the project.

---

#### Enforcement

Behavior that violates this Code of Conduct may result in warnings, removal of content, temporary restrictions, or permanent exclusion from project spaces, depending on the severity and circumstances of the behavior.

Project maintainers are responsible for interpreting and enforcing these standards.

Reports involving conduct concerns should be made privately to the project maintainers when possible.

Reports will be reviewed as fairly and confidentially as reasonably possible.
Good Faith

This Code of Conduct exists to support productive collaboration, not to prevent reasonable technical disagreement or good-faith criticism.
Everyone participating in the NXD project is expected to apply these standards with reasonable judgment and good faith.
